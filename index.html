About Me

A little bit about who I am

Hello everyone, my name is Christian Joseph P. Tongco. I am a second courser student currently studying Computer Science at the University of Bohol.

My first course was Bachelor of Science in Maritime Transportation, and I officially finished the course in late 2025. I chose to study again because Computer Science is the course I really wanted to pursue.

I am a gamer and a nocturnal being who barely sleeps because of gaming, reading, studying, or making school projects. I can play a bit of guitar, I am still studying how to play piano, and I crochet once in a while, especially when I want to give something handmade to someone.

In class, I try my best to help my classmates by giving guidance and encouraging good study habits. I am the eldest in my class today, and I feel honored when classmates come to me for help while I continue working to become a better version of myself.
