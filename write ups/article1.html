My, My President

C.J Tongco

My president is more than the leader of a nation; he is a symbol of the hopes and dreams of the Filipino people. In the Philippines, the president carries the responsibility of guiding more than a hundred million citizens toward progress and unity. For me, “my president” means someone who listens to the people, understands their struggles, and works tirelessly to improve their lives. Leadership is not only about authority but about service, compassion, and accountability. One of the most influential presidents in Philippine history was Manuel L. Quezon. Known as the “Father of the National Language,” he promoted Filipino identity and strengthened the foundation of our nation. His dedication to independence and nation-building reminds me that a president must protect the country’s sovereignty and culture. Through his leadership, he showed that love for country should guide every decision. Another important leader was Corazon Aquino, who became a symbol of democracy after the People Power Revolution. She led the country during a time of political change and restored democratic institutions. Her courage and faith in the Filipino people demonstrated that true leadership is grounded in integrity and trust. From her example, I learn that a president must stand firm in defending freedom and justice. In recent years, presidents like Rodrigo Duterte and Bongbong Marcos have shaped the nation in different ways, each bringing their own vision and priorities. Their leadership styles show how the presidency can influence national policies, public safety, and economic growth. These leaders remind me that every president faces unique challenges and must balance the needs of the people with the realities of governance. Ultimately, “my president” in the Philippines is someone who puts the welfare of Filipinos first. He should value honesty, fairness, and dedication to public service. A true president unites the nation despite differences and leads with both strength and compassion. As a Filipino, I believe my president should inspire pride, protect our democracy, and work toward a brighter future for our country.
