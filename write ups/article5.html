Is My God Your God?

C.J Tongco

As a Catholic, my relationship with God began at home. I learned to pray the Sign of the Cross before meals, to whisper quiet prayers before sleeping, and to trust God during difficult times. My faith is not just something I practice on Sundays—it is part of who I am. Through the Catholic Church, I have come to know a God who is loving, forgiving, and always ready to welcome me back no matter how many times I fall. I grew up knowing Jesus Christ not just as a figure in a book, but as someone close to my heart. When I struggle, I think about His patience. When I fail, I remember His mercy. When I feel lost, I hold on to the belief that He walks with me. My God is not distant or cold; He is personal. He listens when I pray, even when my words are messy and unsure. But as I meet people from different backgrounds and beliefs, I sometimes wonder: Is my God your God? You may pray differently. You may call Him by another name, or understand Him in another way. Maybe you experience Him in silence, in nature, or not at all. These differences used to confuse me, but now they make me curious. I realize that faith is deeply personal, shaped by culture, family, and life experiences. Being Catholic has also taught me that love is at the center of everything. If God is love, then wherever there is real love—kindness, forgiveness, compassion—there is a reflection of Him. Even if our beliefs are not the same, I believe we can still respect each other’s journeys. Faith should never be used to judge or divide, but to bring understanding and peace. So when I ask, “Is My God Your God?” I am not asking to argue or compare. I am asking to connect. I stand firm in my Catholic faith, but I also choose to approach others with humility and an open heart. In the end, I hope that whatever we believe leads us to become better people—more patient, more forgiving, and more loving each day.
